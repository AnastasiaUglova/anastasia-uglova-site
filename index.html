bio

Whatever happened to the cheerful sci-fi future our movies and TV promised us? We're a long way from AI like Data and C-3PO, who help us be us without pretending to be us. The deeper question isn't better guardrails or tougher regulation — it's what we ask AI to optimize for: human thriving, or human imitation?

I'm a technologist and founder building at the intersection of AI, human agency, and relational intelligence — the capacity to drive trust, agency, and real connection. I believe this is what the industry hasn't built yet, and what matters most.

At Better Half, I'm building relational AI that uses decision theory and human-AI psychology to deepen human connection. We're creating the first dataset of longitudinal healthy relating, so we can train AI on empirical signals of human thriving.

I coined the terms "agentic tech" and "tech for human agency" in my research at UT Austin and founded tethics & chill, an invitational community of founders, funders, builders, and thinkers advancing technologies that expand human agency. I also advise ex/ante, the earliest VC fund to back agentic tech.

I write a blog and host the Agentic Tech Podcast.

Previously: AI security at Deloitte, NBC, NPR, the U.S. Senate, founding the Cato Daily Podcast (2006), and six years in East Africa advancing global health, workforce development, and women's education.

For fun: skydiving, acro yoga, ashtanga, climbing, skiing, and reading broadly across science, tech, sci-fi, and theology.
