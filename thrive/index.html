What makes it different

Most coaching stops at the body. I coach the whole system.

I work at the intersection of physical health and relational intelligence — the science of understanding yourself and the people around you. Because feeling good in your body is only half of it. The other half is the calm, the clarity, and the relationships that let you actually enjoy the life you're building.

Good to know

A few straight answers.

Is this fitness coaching or life coaching?

Both — by design. We usually start with the body because it's the fastest way to feel different, then build out into mindset, relationships, and the bigger picture.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. We meet your body exactly where it is and build from there. This is about feeling strong and capable, not about looking a certain way.

What is relational intelligence?

The skill of understanding and navigating relationships — with the people you love and with yourself. It's a huge, often-ignored part of feeling well, and it's at the center of how I coach.

Who do you work with?

Women who are ready to feel strong, clear, and connected — in their body, their relationships, and their work. 1:1 and in small groups.
