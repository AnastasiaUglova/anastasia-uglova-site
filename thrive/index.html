Good to know

A few straight answers.

Do you actually know the health & wellness space?

Deeply. I live the lifestyle and I come from a career in global health. I can speak to your offer, your science, and your client's real hesitations — credibly, without a script.

Have you closed high-ticket before?

I'm new to formal high-ticket closing — not to high-stakes persuasion. I've spent 15+ years earning trust with skeptical stakeholders, executives, and rooms that don't hand it out. That means I ramp faster and execute more responsibly, especially on consultative offers where judgment matters more than a script.

How do you handle objections?

Not by overpowering them. I get curious about the real hesitation under the stated one — usually it's fear, or a gap in belief. Then I help them see what's possible and make the decision that's genuinely right for them. Sometimes that's a confident yes; sometimes an honest no. Both are wins for you.

Are you pushy?

Never. I close consultatively — I bring people to a confident yes, or an honest no. You get committed clients, not a pipeline clogged with bad-fit refunds.

Fractional or full-time?

Either. Bring me in to close a launch or a specific offer, or add me to your team as an ongoing closer. Whatever gets more of the right people to yes.

What kind of offers?

High-ticket health and wellness — premium coaching, programs, memberships, functional-health packages, longevity and recovery. If it's real and it changes lives, I can close it.

Why does relational intelligence matter in sales?

Because closing is relational intelligence: reading what someone really needs, earning trust quickly, and helping them choose what's genuinely good for them. I spent years teaching it — now I close with it.

What's your vibe on a call?

Joyful and possibility-focused. I don't manufacture urgency or poke at wounds. I help people see what's possible to feel genuinely good about saying yes.
