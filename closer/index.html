01

Build trust

02

Locate the pain

Discovery is getting people to open up — honestly, even a little vulnerably — about what's broken and how they want to feel. I listen with empathy, find the alignment that gets to yes, or downsell like it's a steal.

From AI security at Deloitte to enterprise positioning at Notion to executive career coaching for private clients, I make complex, high-stakes offerings make sense to the person whose name goes on the contract. Clarity closes.
